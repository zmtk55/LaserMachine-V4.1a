# Local scene derivatives

Not production-approved. No new inventory is represented.

- outdoor-desktop.webp: compressed derivative of the clean outdoor artwork supplied in this conversation (image-e3f9c041.jpg). The received bytes decode to 1672x941.
- outdoor-mobile.webp: crop of the same artwork, with dark headroom for real HTML controls. This is a reconstruction, not a recovered original vertical asset.
- frio-mobile.webp: UI-free lower scene extracted from frame 0.2 seconds of the supplied 390x844 video. Dark headroom is reconstructed. Original cold desktop/vertical layers are not recovered.
- calor-mobile.webp: UI-free lower scene from image-20bda002.jpg, with reconstructed dark headroom. Original warm desktop/vertical layers are not recovered.

No screenshot controls are rendered into these assets. Original photographs, layered sources and color variants have not all been recovered. The current implementation uses whole-scene transitions/zoom, not recovered three-depth-layer parallax. Color variants are locally derived coating-only recolors, not additional recovered photos or stock promises. Manually traced coating masks exclude steel rims/base and logo badges. Texture and reflections come from the original scene; unmasked RGB pixels remain identical before lossless WebP encoding (verified max difference 0). White/red/other variants are approximate visual previews, not physical color guarantees. Color state is independent of scene state.
